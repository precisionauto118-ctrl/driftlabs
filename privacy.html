Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

What we collect

Your name and email when you create an account. The vehicle details, messages and links you put in an order. Tune files, datalogs and engine codes you upload, email or share with us.

How we use it

To build your tune, support your order and reply to you. We don't sell your information.

Payments

Stripe processes payments. Your card number goes to Stripe, not to us. Stripe shares your name, email and payment status with us so we can match your payment to your order.

Services we rely on

Google Firebase stores accounts, orders, messages and uploaded files. Gmail receives files you email us. Formspree emails us alerts with your name, email, vehicle and message when you order or write to us. GitHub Pages hosts this site, and Google Fonts loads its fonts. These companies may receive technical data such as your IP address and handle it under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and storage

We use browser storage to keep you signed in. We don't run advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

We keep orders and messages for support and business records. You can ask us to delete your account and files at any time, and we'll delete what we aren't required to keep for taxes or legal reasons.

Children

This service is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.
