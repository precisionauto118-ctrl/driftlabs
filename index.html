How it works

Four steps, done over email

No shop visit and no trailer. You drive, log and send files. We do the tuning.

  1. Order & tell us about the vehicle

    Pick your package and fill out the order form with your vehicle, mods, fuel and goals.

  2. Send your files

    Megasquirt: your .msq tune and a datalog. HP Tuners: your stock read file and a scan. We'll show you exactly how.

  3. Get your tune

    We build a tune for your setup and send it back for you to load or flash.

  4. Drive, log, refine

    Send new logs and we send revisions until it hits target and drives clean.

Before you order

What you'll need

For a Megasquirt tune

  • A Megasquirt ECU (MS2, MS3 or MSPNP), installed and able to start and idle the car
  • A wideband O2 sensor wired into the ECU, so your logs record real air/fuel ratios
  • A laptop with TunerStudio to load tunes and record datalogs

For an HP Tuners tune

  • An HP Tuners interface (MPVI3 or newer)
  • Credits to license your vehicle. We'll tell you how many you need.
  • A laptop with VCM Suite (free from HP Tuners) to read, flash and log
  • A wideband is recommended for cammed, boosted or heavily modified engines

FAQ

      
Common questions

    

    

      
Which package do I need?
The Base Tune gets a stock vehicle or a fresh Megasquirt install started and running right. The Daily Tune adds the drivability work a street car needs, like idle, cold start and part throttle, with two revision rounds. The Custom Tune is built around your exact goals, with up to four revision rounds. Built, cammed or boosted vehicles should get the Custom Tune.

      
Can you tune my factory ECU?
For GM cars and trucks, yes. HP Tuners flashes the factory computer. Many older vehicles, like the NA and NB Miata, have factory ECUs that can't be reflashed, so they need a Megasquirt.

      
What vehicles do you tune?
Any vehicle running a Megasquirt ECU, and GM cars and trucks supported by HP Tuners. Not sure if yours qualifies? Choose "Not sure" on the order form, or email us first.

      
Do you do emissions deletes?
No. We don't write tunes that remove or disable emissions equipment such as catalytic converters, EGR or DPF systems.

      
How long does it take?
Tunes usually go out within 3 business days of getting your files. Each revision usually takes 1 to 2 days. Most tunes are finished in 1 to 2 weeks, depending on how quickly you can drive and log.

      
What if I add mods later?
Bigger injectors, a cam, a new turbo or a big change in boost need a re-tune. Small bolt-ons often only need a revision round. Send us the details and we'll tell you which one you need.

      
Do you offer refunds?
If we haven't started your tune, you get a full refund. Once tuning work has started, the fee isn't refundable, but we'll keep working with you until the vehicle runs right on the setup you ordered for.

      
How do I pay?
Checkout runs through Stripe, so you can pay with any major card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Your card details go to Stripe and never touch this site.

      
Is this street legal?
Tunes are sold for off-road and competition use only. Check your state's emissions and inspection rules before driving a modified vehicle on public roads.
