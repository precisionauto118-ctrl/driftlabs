How it works

Four steps, done over email

No shop visit and no trailer. You drive, log and send files. We do the tuning.

  1. Order & tell us about the car

    Fill out the order form with your year, engine, mods, fuel and goals.

  2. Send your files

    Email your current .msq tune and a datalog from TunerStudio. We'll show you exactly how to log.

  3. Get your base map

    We build a map for your setup: fuel, ignition, idle and warmup. You load it in TunerStudio.

  4. Drive, log, refine

    Send new logs and we send revisions until the AFRs land on target and it drives clean.

What you'll need

A Megasquirt ECU

MS2, MS3, or a plug-and-play MSPNP unit, installed and able to start and idle the car.

A wideband O2 sensor

Wired into the ECU so your datalogs record real air/fuel ratios. Narrowband sensors can't be used to tune.

A laptop with TunerStudio

To load maps and record datalogs. The free version works. MS Registered is nicer to use.

FAQ

      
Common questions

    

    

      
Can you tune my stock ECU?
No. NA and NB factory ECUs can't be reflashed the way newer cars can. You'll need a Megasquirt, and an MSPNP unit is the easiest swap since it plugs into the factory harness.

      
How long does it take?
Base maps usually go out within 3 business days of getting your files. Each revision usually takes 1 to 2 days. Most tunes are finished in 1 to 2 weeks, depending on how quickly you can drive and log.

      
What if I add mods later?
Bigger injectors, a new turbo or a big change in boost need a re-tune. Small bolt-ons often only need a revision round. Send us the details and we'll tell you which one you need.

      
Do you offer refunds?
If we haven't started your base map, you get a full refund. Once tuning work has started, the fee isn't refundable, but we'll keep working with you until the car runs right on the setup you ordered for.

      
How do I pay?
Checkout runs through Stripe, so you can pay with any major card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Your card details go to Stripe and never touch this site.

      
Is this street legal?
Tunes are sold for off-road and competition use only. Check your state's emissions and inspection rules before driving a modified car on public roads.
