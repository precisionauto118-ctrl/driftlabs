Before you order

What you'll need

For a Megasquirt tune

  • A Megasquirt ECU (MS2, MS3 or MSPNP), installed and able to start and idle the car
  • A wideband O2 sensor wired into the ECU, so your logs record real air/fuel ratios
  • A laptop with TunerStudio to load tunes and record datalogs

For an HP Tuners tune

  • An HP Tuners interface (MPVI3 or newer)
  • Credits to license your vehicle. We'll tell you how many you need.
  • A laptop with VCM Suite (free from HP Tuners) to read, flash and log
  • A code scan. We ask for any current engine codes before we tune.
  • A wideband is recommended for cammed, boosted or heavily modified engines

FAQ

Common questions

Which package do I need?

The Base Tune gets a stock vehicle or a fresh Megasquirt install started and running right. The Daily Tune adds the drivability work a street car needs, like idle, cold start and part throttle. The Custom Tune is built around your exact goals, with up to four revision rounds. Built, cammed or boosted vehicles should get the Custom Tune.

Can you tune my factory ECU?

For GM cars and trucks, yes. HP Tuners flashes the factory computer. Many older vehicles, like the NA and NB Miata, have factory ECUs that can't be reflashed, so they need a Megasquirt.

What vehicles do you tune?

Any vehicle running a Megasquirt ECU, and GM cars and trucks supported by HP Tuners. Not sure if yours qualifies? Choose "Not sure" on the order form and tell us what you have.

Do you do AFM / DFM (DOD) deletes?

Yes, on GM cars, trucks and SUVs, for racing use only. We can turn off cylinder deactivation in the tune, or tune a vehicle that has a full AFM/DFM delete kit (lifters, cam and valley cover) installed. List your parts in the order form.

Do you do emissions deletes?

No. We don't write tunes that remove or disable emissions equipment such as catalytic converters, EGR or DPF systems.

How long does it take?

Your tune usually goes out within a few hours of getting your files, and each revision within a few hours of getting your new logs. How long the whole process takes mostly depends on how quickly you can drive and log.

Can a tune hurt my engine?

We build every tune conservatively, with your engine's safety first, and check it against your own logs. Engine trouble usually traces back to something mechanical, like worn parts, low fuel pressure, vacuum leaks or bad sensors. That's why we ask about codes and known problems before we start. A tune can't repair a mechanical problem, so your vehicle needs to be in good shape first.

What if I add mods later?

Bigger injectors, a cam, a new turbo or a big change in boost need a re-tune. Small bolt-ons often only need a revision round. Send us the details and we'll tell you which one you need.

Do you offer refunds?

No. All purchases are final and we don't offer refunds.

How do I pay?

Checkout runs through Stripe, so you can pay with any major card, Apple Pay, Cash App Pay or Amazon Pay, or pay over time with Klarna if you qualify. Your card details go to Stripe and never touch this site.

Is this street legal?

Tunes are sold for off-road and competition use only. Check your state's emissions and inspection rules before driving a modified vehicle on public roads.
